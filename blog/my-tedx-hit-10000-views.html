My TEDx Hit 10,000 Views!

The Yay I've been hoping for finally happened after all - my TEDx talk hit 10,000 views! 🥹🙌🏽🎉

Apparently only 3% of TEDx talks reach over 10,000 views, so this. Feels. WILD.

THANK YOU SO MUCH to everyone who watched, commented, shared, and replied to my messages - I've heard from people from all across my life the last few weeks. Some of whom I've always been close with, and some of whom supported it even though we haven't been in touch for years.

I've been so lucky to share my excitement with so many people! 🥹🫶🏽

I'm so thankful my message is spreading, and I'm hoping it keeps reaching the people who need to hear it:

  • Those who are living with an invisible struggle that's outside of their control
  • Those with something about themselves or their lives that they're hesitant to share with others
  • And even those just having a bad day

Everyone can benefit from finding and creating their own joy to get through their difficult times - and this talk shows you how.

If you haven't seen it yet, you can watch, comment and share by clicking on the YouTube link here - thank you so much! 🙏🏽💖✨
