My TEDx Talk Crossed 20,000 Views!

Squealed at home alone in my living room yesterday because my TEDx talk "How can we fit in without losing who we are?" reached a new milestone - it's hit over 20,000 views! 🥹🎉

Back in June, while wearing an outfit that felt like sunshine and with my parents, Dan and best friends in the audience, I was nervous and excited ALL DAY.

I was so thankful to have the support of the best Yay Squad, and I'm so happy to see my talk reaching so many people that might need to hear it.

THANK YOU SO MUCH to everyone who watched, commented, and shared it so far - I'm so thankful my message is spreading, and I'm hoping it keeps reaching the people who need to hear it:

  • Those who are living with an invisible struggle that's outside of their control
  • Those with something about themselves or their lives that they're hesitant to share with others
  • Those who are hiding or masking to fit in, and wondering where to go from here

Everyone can benefit from finding and creating their own joy to get through their difficult times - and this talk shows you how.

If you haven't seen my TEDx yet, you can watch, comment and share at the YouTube link here - thank you so much! 🫶🏽✨
