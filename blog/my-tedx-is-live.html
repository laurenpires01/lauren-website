My TEDx Talk Is Live on YouTube!

The way I SQUEALED when I found it - my TEDx talk is now out in the world! 🥹

I'm not usually a call-to-action kind of person, but it would mean SO much to me if you could watch, comment on and share my talk, "How can we fit in without losing who we are?" ✨

It truly feels like it encapsulates my journey of never feeling "normal," to accepting my Thing that makes me feel different, and realizing everyone else has their own Thing too.

But finally accepting your Thing isn't actually the solution to finding happiness in your life. This talk shares what is.

You can watch, comment and share by clicking on the YouTube link here - thank you so much! 💖🙏🏽✨
