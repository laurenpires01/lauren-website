I'm a TEDx Speaker!

Yesterday's daily yay was a big one - I stood on the red dot wearing an outfit that felt like sunshine, and became a TEDx speaker at TEDxMississauga: Ripple Effects!

I'm still going through photos and videos and messages (and I'm so thankful to have so much support 🥹) so I'm sure I'll have more thoughts to share, but for now -

Thank you to my parents and my boyfriend Dan for being the original ripple effects in getting me to share my journey 💖

Thank you to my best friends for always showing up AND hyping me up for everything I do 🫶🏽

Thank you to Brian Miller and Francisco Mahfuz for all the support leading up to the talk 🙏🏽

And thank you to the TEDxMississauga team for having me!

And if you were there for the show, somehow I didn't say it at all afterwards so I'll say it now - thank you for coming to my TED(x) talk. 🥹🔴🙌🏽
