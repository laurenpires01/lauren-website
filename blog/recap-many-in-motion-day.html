Recap: Many in Motion Day With the Rick Hansen Foundation School Program

My probable last Rick Hansen Foundation presentation of the school year today was a special one - celebrating Many in Motion Day and National AccessAbility Week, for the 40th anniversary of Rick Hansen's Man in Motion World Tour, at Rick Hansen Secondary School!

I spoke to the whole school across two presentations, and had some super engaged students in both. After I thanked one of the grade 9/10 students for being so interactive, he said he was really interested in all the content, which was so great to hear 🎉

Today's presentation likely ends my first full year as a School Program Ambassador, and I'm looking forward to what the next school year brings! ✨
