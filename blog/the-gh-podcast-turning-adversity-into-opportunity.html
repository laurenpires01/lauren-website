Talking Career Surprises and the Daily Yay on The GH Podcast

"I used to think that success was accomplishing whatever you set out to do, and I realized that success is really celebrating the growth along the journey that maybe you didn't expect."

Had such a fun conversation with one of the staff members at the University of Guelph-Humber on my career journey of two jobs I never thought I'd have (festival events and disability speaker), my TEDx talk (which was at 18,000 views at the time, and is now approaching 45,000!) and of course, the daily yay!

So cool to go back to the campus I was attending when I was finally diagnosed with my neuromuscular disorder. You can listen to the episode on Spotify here, or look it up on Apple Podcasts! Yay! ✨
