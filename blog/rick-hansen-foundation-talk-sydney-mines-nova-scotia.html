Speaking Virtually to a Grade 9 Class in Sydney Mines, Nova Scotia

Looking forward to speaking virtually to a grade 9 class in Sydney Mines, Nova Scotia today with a presentation on accessibility and barriers, on behalf of the Rick Hansen Foundation School Program!

For the first time that I've seen, the teacher sent a whole list of questions from the students before the presentation, and it's so cool to see they're already so engaged in Rick Hansen's work and accessibility in their own environments.

Thankful to the team at the Rick Hansen Foundation for their support! 🙏🏽✨
