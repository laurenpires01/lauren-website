🦽 Muscular Dystrophy Canada · May 24, 2026

Walk & Roll

Lauren is the 2026 Toronto Walk Champion, leading The Yay Team at Ashbridges Bay Park.

Lauren Pires was named the 2026 Toronto Walk Champion for Muscular Dystrophy Canada. She lives with Central Core Disease, a rare neuromuscular disorder, and May 24 will be the first time she walks a 5K surrounded by people who share her family's medical journey. The walk also launches MDC's national What About Canada? policy campaign.

💜 Rare Disease Day · February 28

#LightUpForRare

A national campaign to light Canadian landmarks in rare disease colours on Rare Disease Day.

Lauren organized the #LightUpForRare campaign in partnership with the Canadian Organization for Rare Disorders (CORD), coordinating with municipal governments and landmark operators to light up monuments across Canada for Rare Disease Day 2026. The campaign received national media coverage including Breakfast Television and CityNews.
