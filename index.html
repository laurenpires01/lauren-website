80%

of disabilities are ones you can't see.

That means people living with them are on your team right now, deciding every day whether it's safe to say so.

Lauren knows that decision well. Born with Central Core Disease, a rare neuromuscular condition that leaves her with about 33% of the average person's strength, she hid it for 30 years. Her keynotes give organizations the words and the culture to make telling the truth feel safe.

Meet Lauren

Lauren Pires went undiagnosed for two decades and spent 30 years hiding her disability. Today she is one of Canada's most visible voices for people living with invisible and rare disabilities, and she speaks to corporations, governments and schools about bias, belonging and finding joy on hard days.

  • #2 Inspirational Speaker of the Year, Speaker Slam 2023
  • D-30 Disability Impact List, Diversability 2024
  • First Canadian to receive the "But You LOOK Good" Inspiration Award
  • Canada's first Invisible Disabilities Association Ambassador
  • Led #LightUpForRare, lighting 26 landmarks across 18 Canadian cities
